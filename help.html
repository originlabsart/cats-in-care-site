01 Volunteer

A pair of hands.

Ask how you can help, whether that is your time, a spare room or a pair of hands at a sale.

We will not list jobs here that we cannot promise. Call us, tell us what you could offer, and we will tell you where it would help most. If you could look after a cat in your home for a while, ask us about fostering.

Call to volunteer

02 Donate

Give to the cats.

Gifts support our rescue and homing work. We do not take donations through this website: there is no online form or payment page here.

  • By phone

    Call us on 07449 370133 and we will arrange your gift with you.

  • By post

    Write to us at 24 Redland Lane, Westbury, Wiltshire BA13 3QA, and we will be in touch about your gift.

  • At a sale

    Come along to one of our local sales and fundraisers. Ask us when the next one is.

Gift Aid

If you pay UK tax, ask us whether your gift can be Gift Aided.

Gifts in kind

Some people like to give things rather than money. Ask us what’s needed before you bring anything, so it goes where it helps.

Or give in crypto.

Cats in Care also takes gifts in Solana. Scan the code with your wallet app or copy the address. Please send on the Solana network only: a gift sent on the wrong one can't be got back.

03 Sales and fundraisers

Come to a sale.

We hold local sales and fundraisers to raise money for the cats.

We do not publish dates here. Ask us when the next one is, whether we could use items for it, or whether we need a hand on the day.

04 Fundraise for us

Got an idea?

If you would like to raise money for the cats, a bake sale, a sponsored walk on the downs, anything at all, please ask us before you start. We will help you get it right and make sure the money reaches the cats.

Talk to us first

05 Gifts in wills

Remembering the cats.

Gifts to UK charities in a will are usually free of Inheritance Tax. If you are thinking of leaving a gift to the cats, these are the details your solicitor will need:

06 Questions

Before you help.

Anything else, ring us on 07449 370133.

Can I donate online?

Not through this website. Call us on 07449 370133 or write to us, and we will arrange your gift with you.

Can my gift be Gift Aided?

If you pay UK tax, ask us whether your gift can be Gift Aided.

What items do you need?

Ask us what’s needed before you bring anything. That way your gift goes where it helps.

When is the next sale?

Get in touch and we will tell you. We do not publish dates on this site.

Can I volunteer if I can only spare a little time?

Ask us. Tell us what you could offer and we will tell you where it would help.

Where does the money go?

Into the rescue and homing of unwanted and abandoned cats and kittens. Our latest income and spending, from the public register, are on our about page.
